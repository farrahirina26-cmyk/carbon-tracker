Help & Documentation

Find answers to common questions about tracking your carbon footprint and using the application.

This application helps you monitor, calculate, and analyze your daily environmental impact based on your everyday commutes and home electricity or appliance usage, empowering you to make eco-friendly choices.

Trip carbon emissions are calculated by multiplying the travel distance (in kilometers) by the specific emission factor of the transport type you used (such as Bus, Car, CNG, etc.).

Your home footprint is calculated based on the count and daily usage hours of your electrical appliances like lights, fans, ACs, and electronic devices, using predefined energy consumption and emission factors.

The Eco Score is a daily rating (starting from a baseline of 100) that reflects how environmentally friendly your daily activities are. Lower carbon emissions keep your score higher.

Your eco score goes down as your travel carbon emissions increase (deducting points based on total CO2 generated). Choosing eco-friendly transport like walking or public transit helps maintain a higher score.

Higher electricity usage and longer appliance operating hours increase your total home CO2 emissions, which subsequently reduces your daily Eco Score.

The application currently supports multiple transport types including Walking, Rickshaw, Bus, Car, and CNG, each with its own specific emission factor.
